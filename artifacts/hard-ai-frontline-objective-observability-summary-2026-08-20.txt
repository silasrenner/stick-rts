Exact frontline definition
--------------------------
For each team, inspect only living friendly non-miner combat units (warrior,
archer, hero). Direction is toward the opposing home:
- Player/Left attacks toward increasing x.
- AI/Right attacks toward decreasing x.

forwardMostFriendlyCombatX is the friendly combat unit with greatest progress in
that direction. The active frontline is the inclusive 420-world-pixel band
behind that unit, toward the team's home. It reports:
- frontline.friendlyCombatCount
- frontline.friendlyPower
- frontline.visibleEnemyCombatCount
- frontline.visibleEnemyPower
- frontline.centerX / forwardMostFriendlyCombatX

Visible enemy combat is local only when it is within +/-420 world pixels of the
friendly forward-most position. This produces a symmetric, renderer-free,
non-historical front definition. Power uses the existing assessment convention:
max HP + (damage * 5).

Exact objective-progress definition
-----------------------------------
Progress is the normalized directed position of the forward-most living friendly
combat unit between homes:
  0 at own home
  1 at enemy home
and is clamped to [0, 1]. distanceRemaining is raw world distance from that
position to the enemy home. With no living friendly combat unit, forward-most
and distanceRemaining are null, counts/power are zero, and progress is neutral
0.

Information boundary
--------------------
Friendly combat state is authoritative. Enemy frontline values use ONLY
memory.currentlyVisibleEnemies, which is populated by the existing shared
current team-vision query. Remembered mobile snapshots are intentionally
excluded: a remembered enemy at an old x cannot become a current frontline
contact. No vision or controlled-memory semantics changed.

Conclusion and next heuristic direction
----------------------------------------
The primitives clearly explain the stalled/dispersed attacks that global combat
count concealed. Frontline strength identifies whether apparent army mass has
arrived together; objective progress distinguishes a shallow exchange from a
push approaching the defensive line. They are distinct and both should remain
available to later reusable heuristics.

Recommended next step (NOT IMPLEMENTED): introduce two separate reusable
heuristic terms before any combined momentum/history system:
1. relative frontline strength = friendly frontline power versus CURRENTLY
   visible frontline enemy power; and
2. objective progress / objective-distance pressure.

Keep them raw and independently weighted by goals only after a separately
approved behavior pass. Do not introduce engagementMomentum, casualty windows,
damage ledgers, special-case fallback rules, or threshold tuning yet.
