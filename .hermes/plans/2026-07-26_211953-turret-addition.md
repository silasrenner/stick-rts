# Turret Addition Implementation Plan

> **For Hermes:** Execute only after Silas approves the plan; use direct, visible progress updates. Do not delegate or change workflow without prior discussion.

**Goal:** Add a purchasable, automatic base-defense turret that costs 450 gold, takes 20 seconds to build, uses five population-cap slots, is limited to two per team, and fires for 28 damage.

**Architecture:** Turrets will be stationary, destroyable combat entities stored with defensive structures but excluded from the supply-cap bonus. They use the existing projectile and target-selection system, receive two deterministic positions just beyond the mine, and the defending formation is moved beyond that turret line.

**Tech Stack:** Browser ES modules, fixed-timestep JS simulation, Canvas rendering, CDP regression scripts.

---

## Agreed product specification

- Build-bar item label: `Turret`.
- Price: **450 gold**.
- Build time: **20 seconds**, equal to a Structure.
- Capacity cost: **5 population-cap slots** per turret.
- Limit: **two per team**, counting completed and queued turrets.
- Combat: **28 damage per shot** (2× Archer), 1-second cooldown, 280 range, 320 projectile speed, 180 HP.
- Placement: automatic, just beyond the mine toward the battlefield, using `TURRET_SLOT_OFFSETS`.
- Defensive formation: combat units line up beyond all completed turret positions; Warriors occupy the exposed/front line, Archers remain behind Warriors but not behind turrets.
- Turrets are stationary defense; they do not provide supply capacity.
- The AI does not buy turrets in this initial scope. The player can buy them; Watch AI exposes the simulation behavior only if turrets are introduced through later AI policy work.

## Risks and decisions intentionally deferred

- Hard AI will not buy turrets in this first version; adding it would require a distinct strategic policy and balance pass.
- There is no manual turret placement in scope; automatic slots avoid new touch/mouse placement UX and preserve determinism.
- Turret health/range are initial tuning values. Physical preview feedback should decide whether 180 HP / 280 range are fair before release.
