# Sunmeadow League — Style Lock v01

## Inputs
- Visual benchmark: `docs/creative-direction/reference/sunmeadow-league.jpg`
- Content contract: `docs/creative-direction/creative-direction-primer-v0.1.md`

## Prompt
Create an original polished pixel-art faction keyframe for the Sunmeadow League in Stick RTS, matching the supplied Sunmeadow benchmark’s visual quality, pixel density, warm daylight, and cheerful civic fantasy scale without copying its exact pixels, characters, or architecture. In a bright grassland setting, show a compact readable side-view base group: a pale-stone mine with oak supports and lantern, a rounded green-roof watchtower with a visible banner, a warm timber-and-stone Hearthhall with supplies and chimney smoke, and a luminous civic Sunspire Core with layered towers and a visible magical center. Include four small, gameplay-readable silhouettes: a friendly miner with bronze helmet/pick/lantern, a warrior with a round shield, an archer with longbow and short cloak, and one heroic banner-bearing leader. Shapes are rounded, upright, open, organized, and optimistic. Use cream stone, meadow green, warm gold, cobalt accents, oak timber, bronze, painted cloth, sun/wheat/lantern motifs. Crisp intentional pixel clusters, polished 16-bit-level execution, no UI/text/logos, no grimdark tone, no generic faceless army.

## Output constraints
- clear side-view game-art presentation, not portrait art
- units and buildings must read at small scale
- flat/clean enough backgrounds around subjects to support later sprite production
- no text, watermark, UI, or logos

## Candidate review questions
1. Is the civic/friendly Sunmeadow identity obvious before fine detail?
2. Are every unit role and building type readable?
3. Does it hit the supplied benchmark bar instead of looking like generic pixel fantasy?
