# Open Frontier — Style Lock v01

## Inputs
- Visual benchmark: `docs/creative-direction/reference/open-frontier-benchmark-crop.jpg`
- Content contract: `docs/creative-direction/creative-direction-primer-v0.1.md`

## Prompt
Create an original, polished pixel-art wide establishing frame for a side-view fantasy RTS named Stick RTS. It must meet the quality and composition bar of the supplied Open Frontier benchmark without reproducing its exact objects or pixels. Show a bright, welcoming frontier under a broad blue sky with large soft clouds, layered blue mountains, rolling green hills, a distant tree line, a shallow stream or pond, a single weathered stone watchtower ruin, sparse wooden fences and road markers, and grass/wildflowers along the foreground edges. Keep the central lower combat lane broad, flat, uncluttered, and easy to read. The feeling is heroic, warm, adventurous, and inhabited—not grimdark, photorealistic, muddy, painterly, or vector-like. Use crisp intentional pixel clusters with polished 16-bit-level detail, controlled bright palette, strong depth separation, and no UI, text, logos, or copied characters.

## Output constraints
- 16:9 wide landscape composition
- no units or buildings in the lane
- foreground, midground, and background clearly separable for later layer production
- no text, watermark, UI, or logos

## Candidate review questions
1. Does it meet the supplied benchmark’s density and pixel-art finish?
2. Does the combat lane remain unmistakably readable?
3. Does it feel like an inviting large frontier rather than a generic background?
