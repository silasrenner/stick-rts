# Bramblecrest Wardens — Style Lock v01

## Inputs
- Visual benchmark: *pending a supplied Bramblecrest source master*
- Content contract: `docs/creative-direction/creative-direction-primer-v0.1.md`

## Prompt
Create an original polished pixel-art faction keyframe for the Bramblecrest Wardens in Stick RTS, matching the supplied Bramblecrest benchmark’s visual quality, pixel density, grounded material richness, and strong silhouette hierarchy without copying its exact pixels, characters, or architecture. In a bright frontier woodland-hill setting, show a compact readable side-view base group: a rugged rock-cut iron mine with timber beams, lanterns and carts; a fortified stone-and-timber Warden Tower with defensive platform and stag banner; a low sturdy Warden Hall with supplies, carved posts and protective fencing; and a Runewood Core built around glowing standing stones, carved runes, timber architecture and restrained vegetation. Include four small gameplay-readable silhouettes: a miner with iron helmet/pick/lantern, a broad warrior with sturdy shield, an archer/ranger with hood and practical bow, and one commanding stag/rune leader. Shapes are sturdier, grounded, slightly angular, resilient and crafted—not evil, savage, horror-themed, or overly dark. Use pine green, brick red, warm brown, iron gray, pale stone, leather and rune/stag/thorn/pine motifs. Crisp intentional pixel clusters, polished 16-bit-level execution, no UI/text/logos.

## Output constraints
- clear side-view game-art presentation, not portrait art
- units and buildings must read at small scale
- flat/clean enough backgrounds around subjects to support later sprite production
- no text, watermark, UI, or logos

## Candidate review questions
1. Does Bramblecrest read as defensive and land-conscious while remaining sympathetic?
2. Is it visibly distinct from Sunmeadow in silhouette and material before color alone?
3. Does it achieve the benchmark bar without becoming grimdark or a generic forest faction?
